## Astrophysics PhD
Gravitational lensing of the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB).
### Detect new gravitational effect - *lensing rotation*
([paper](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1475-7516/2023/08/048){:target='_blank'})
 - **Statistical Estimator:** I mathematically derived a unique statistical estimator for *lensing rotation* that leveraged 
correlations between different astronomical datasets.
 - **Forecasts:** I used the Fisher Information matrix to forecast the success of my estimator.

Using my estimator, *lensing rotation* is detectable within 2 years instead of 20; 
saving billions on telescope costs.

### Analysis pipeline and simulations
([paper](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1475-7516/2025/02/034){:target='_blank'})
 - **Pipeline:** I implemented an end-to-end Python pipeline, with modular steps, that applies my estimator to input data.
   - Pipeline includes pre-processing, filtering, Fourier transforms, and data validation steps.
 - **Simulations:** I created mock datasets from large N-body cosmology simulations to validate the pipeline, and my estimator, on a high-performance supercomputer (HPC).

Verified that my estimator is robust against realistic data complications, noise, and uncertainties. 
Pipeline can be re-used with future data for scientific research.
